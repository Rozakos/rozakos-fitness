## Listing copy

**App name**

Rozakos Fitness

**Short description**

Log strength workouts, track progress, and connect your own training devices.

**Full description**

Rozakos Fitness is a focused strength-training log for planning sessions, recording sets,
and seeing progress without ads or social clutter.

Build reusable routines with target sets and rep ranges, then log weight, reps, warm-ups,
RPE or RIR as you train. See previous values beside each exercise, manage rest periods, use
the plate calculator, and reorder or swap movements without interrupting the workout.

After training, review session volume and personal records. Exercise history, estimated 1RM
trends, weekly volume, muscle-group breakdowns, and bodyweight tracking make progress visible
over time. Bodyweight movements support added load and assisted weight.

You choose how your data works:

- Use local mode with no account, keeping your fitness log on that phone.
- Create an account to sync with the Rozakos Fitness server, keep recent cloud results available
  offline, merge history previously recorded in local mode, and connect your own authorized
  equipment, such as a Raspberry Pi rep counter, through personal API keys.
- Delete your account and associated server data at any time from inside the app.

Rozakos Fitness contains no advertising or third-party analytics. It is a training log, not
a medical service, and does not provide medical advice.

**Release notes for 1.9.0**

Play-ready release with new Rozakos Fitness branding, privacy and account controls, email

routine, progress, bodyweight, and device-integration features.
